## NEW AI/ML CAPABILITIES



L3Harris successfully demonstrated integration of its WESCAM MX™-Series electro-optical/infrared (EO/IR) imaging systems with Palantir Technologies Inc. Sensor Inference Platform (SIP), which provides artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) capabilities on edge computing platforms. SIP successfully provided an orchestration container for multiple AI/ML models for advanced target detection and delineation.



The demonstration took place in Loveland, Colorado where L3Harris and Palantir engineers outfitted a fixed wing aircraft with L3Harris' state-of-the-art WESCAM MX™-20 EO/IR system coupled with Palantir software. Palantir's SIP provided advanced object detection, tracking and object classification capability.  In turn, providing advanced capability  for expanded operator and system situational awareness, target identification and cross-domain communication to increase operational effectiveness in an ever evolving and complex threat environment.



Combining L3Harris' high-performance imaging systems with Palantir's AI-enabled automation provides operators, aboard all platforms, with a significant advantage in agile decision-making, over traditional optical solutions. This future-ready sensor optimization through a specialized user interface reduces workload, alerting operators of threats that require immediate action.



'This highly successful demonstration with Palantir showcases L3Harris' ability to remain at the forefront of cutting edge innovation,' said Tom Kirkland, Vice President and General Manager, Global Optical Systems, L3Harris. 'Working with Palantir provides us with an unprecedented opportunity to provide customers with the most advanced solutions to meet their critical mission needs.'



L3Harris' WESCAM MX-20 EO/IR imaging system is part of a comprehensive airborne, ground and maritime portfolio of multi-sensor, multi-spectral systems deployed worldwide, at varying ranges, and with overlapping fields-of-view, creating the opportunity for uninterrupted collection of intelligence through optical surveillance and targeting of threats on the battlefield. L3Harris' WESCAM MX-Series of systems are on over 270 different types of platforms across all domains.



The integration of these technologies is a prime example of L3Harris' role as the Trusted Disruptor in the defence industry, committed to collaborating in unique ways with other innovative companies throughout the defence technology ecosystem to enhance the capabilities we deliver to our warfighters.
